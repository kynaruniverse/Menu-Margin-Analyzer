MENU MARGIN ANALYZER — CHANGELOG
=================================

Version 1.0
-----------
Initial release.

Included:

- Menu screen: add, edit, confirm and remove dishes. Dishes are drafts
  until confirmed — drafts don't affect your numbers.
- Summary screen: total revenue, ingredient cost, gross profit, overall
  food cost, and menu benchmarks (average sales, margin, profit per
  sale, total profit per dish).
- Profit Map screen: a visual scatter map plotting every confirmed dish
  by relative sales and margin, split into four categories —
  Strong, Grow, Optimise, Review.
- Opportunities screen: every dish running a higher food cost than your
  menu average, ranked by potential monthly and annual improvement,
  plus a "Biggest Opportunity" panel on the Summary screen.
- What-if scenarios: per-dish testing of price changes, ingredient cost
  changes, and sales volume changes, each showing the projected
  monthly and annual impact.
- Sample menu: a six-dish example menu to explore the tool immediately,
  shown automatically on first launch.
- CSV import and export, and a printable report covering all four
  screens regardless of which one is on screen when you print.
- All data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded
  anywhere.
- index.html is fully self-contained (styling and functionality are
  built in), so it keeps working even if it's copied or moved on its
  own, separately from the rest of the app folder.

Note: the What-if Scenario tools were originally planned for a later
update but shipped as part of this first release.

Nothing to report yet for future versions — check back here after
your next download for what's new.
